How these numbers are calculated

All collection cards show lifetime data, including entries without dates and media types hidden from your sidebar. Period controls affect the monthly charts only. A title is unique within a media type and catalogue source; different editions or sources can appear separately.

Game time is recorded in minutes. Repeated rows for the same game use the highest cumulative value to avoid counting a Steam total twice. Unmatched connected games are included in the playtime headline. Completed movies contribute one runtime per tracked watch. TV counts each episode watch once, including rewatches and specials; season totals are an alternate view of those same watches. Anime uses watched episode count × catalogue average duration. Unknown runtimes are excluded and coverage is shown.

Books, manga, comics and board games use pages, chapters, issues and plays. Yamtrack does not record reading or board-game session duration, so these are not converted into fictional hours. Written notes are counted separately from ratings; they are not necessarily reviews.

Monthly consumption uses dated watches or positive progress changes recorded after an entry’s initial import. It reflects recording dates, not a recovered Steam session history. Resetting progress and adding it again records another increment. Collection additions include imports and therefore are not evidence of playing or watching on that day. Catalogue details refresh in the background at most once per week; errors are shown in coverage.
